How it works

Four steps to closing.

  1. Pricing visit

    I walk the house and give you a written price with the comparable sales I used. There is no charge.

  2. Prep and photos

    A short list of inexpensive fixes worth making, then professional photos paid for by the brokerage.

  3. On the market

    Listed on the MLS and the major home sites. You hear from me after every showing.

  4. Offers to closing

    I check each buyer's financing and terms, answer repair requests with real costs, and see it through to closing.
