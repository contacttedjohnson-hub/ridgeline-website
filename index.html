Why Ridgeline

Four things most agents can't tell you. I can.

Buying a house is a construction question, a money question, and a negotiation. I've done all three for a living.

01

What the house will actually cost you

I've renovated four houses with my own money. I know what a tired roof, an old panel, or a settled slab costs to fix, and I'll tell you before you write an offer.

I am not a licensed home inspector, and I'll always recommend you hire one. What I add is a renovator's eye on the walk-through, so the inspection confirms what we already expect.

02

Negotiation that comes from the numbers

I negotiate leases, vendor contracts and purchase prices every week as part of managing a large commercial property. Your offer will be built on comps and repair costs, not on hope.

03

Financing and credit, explained plainly

If your credit isn't where a lender wants it yet, I can walk you through what actually moves a score and how long it takes. I understand underwriting, and I'll help you get lender-ready before we go shopping.

04

Title and closing without surprises

Liens, easements, survey issues, closing statements. I read the title work myself and I'll explain every line of your closing disclosure before you sign it.
